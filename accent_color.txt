Press Win + R, type regedit, and press Enter to open the Registry Editor.

Navigate to the following path: HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Themes\Personalize.

Locate the ColorPrevalence key in the right pane.

Double-click on ColorPrevalence and set its value to 1 to enable the accent color on the Taskbar and Start Menu.

Navigate to: HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Themes\Accent.

Find the AccentColor key and double-click it.

Enter the desired color value in hexadecimal format (e.g., FF0000 for red) and click OK.

Restart your computer or log out and log back in to apply the changes.
